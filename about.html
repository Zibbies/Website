About me

I connect the customer experience with the operation behind it.

My career has moved from frontline customer interaction into team leadership, service operations, process improvement, customer insight and cross-functional problem solving.

01

I start with the customer.

A customer issue can reveal a product problem, process gap, training need, ownership problem, capacity constraint or a larger pattern. I want to understand which one I am looking at before deciding what to change.

02

I think in systems.

Strong CX depends on more than frontline effort. It requires clear ownership, usable processes, good information, product partnership, thoughtful workforce planning and a feedback loop that actually closes.

03

I care about retention and value.

I think about customer health, adoption, repeat demand, service quality and the moments that influence whether customers stay, grow and advocate.

04

I use improvement methods practically.

I use journey mapping, Pareto analysis, 5 Whys, DMAIC, process mapping and FMEA as working tools, not as labels. The goal is a better customer and business outcome.
