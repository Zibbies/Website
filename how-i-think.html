How I think

I do not see CX as a support function alone.

I see it as a cross-functional operating discipline connecting customer insight, people, product, process, technology and commercial outcomes.

01

Reactive → proactive

The goal is not simply to answer more tickets faster. I want to identify the conditions that create avoidable demand and intervene earlier.

02

Ticket → relationship

A ticket is an event. A customer has a history. I use account context and customer health to understand the relationship behind individual contacts.

03

Feedback → action

Collecting feedback is not the finish line. The value comes from identifying patterns, assigning ownership, making changes and closing the loop with customers.

04

Metric → decision

I want every important metric to answer a management question. If a metric does not change a decision, I question why we are tracking it.

Improvement loop

Listen → Learn → Act → Communicate → Measure.

I use the loop repeatedly, because customer operations should learn faster than the problems around them change.

A principle I use

I do not believe every customer problem needs a bigger team.

Sometimes the best CX intervention is removing the reason the customer needed the team in the first place.
