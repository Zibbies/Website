Portfolio case study

PropTech CX Transformation

Designing a customer operation that moves from reactive ticket handling to proactive account management.

The context

I used a simulated assessment dataset representing approximately 600 communities across two countries, with 5,010 tickets over 12 weeks. This is a fictional assessment case and does not contain confidential employer or customer information.

The problem

The diagnostic showed inconsistent service performance, unallocated communities, Engineering escalation friction and concentrated recurring demand.

How I approached it

I mapped the customer journey, examined demand and priority patterns, introduced intelligent routing, defined proactive account ownership, strengthened Engineering governance and created a continuous improvement cadence.

Proactive account health

I would monitor repeat contacts, reopened issues, unresolved escalations, recurring product failures, engagement changes, renewal proximity and account-specific service patterns.

Engineering handoff

My model is customer signal → frontline diagnosis → known issue check → technical evidence → Engineering escalation → acceptance criteria → resolution → customer communication → root-cause learning.

What I would measure

Operational measures include SLA performance, backlog, handling time and escalation ageing. Customer measures include satisfaction, repeat contacts and account health. Business measures include retention, renewal health and expansion opportunities.

My key learning: a ticket is an event. An account has history, relationship and future value.
