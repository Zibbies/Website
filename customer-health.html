Customer Health & Lifecycle Value

Moving customer management from today's satisfaction to tomorrow's relationship health.

My principle

A customer can be satisfied today and still be at risk tomorrow. That is why I look at the wider customer relationship, not only the latest interaction.

The lifecycle

Acquire → Onboard → Adopt → Engage → Retain → Expand → Advocate.

Health signals

I consider support demand, repeat contacts, unresolved issues, product engagement, adoption patterns, stakeholder engagement, service friction and renewal proximity.

Customer management

Health signals should change the operating response. A healthy account may need advocacy and expansion support, while a weakening account may need intervention, education, product partnership or executive attention.

Long-term value

I connect experience with retention, renewal health, adoption and expansion opportunities. I avoid treating customer value as a single isolated metric.

My focus

I want teams to know which customers need attention, why they need it and what action should happen next.
