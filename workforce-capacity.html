CX Workforce & Capacity

Building coverage around demand, complexity and customer risk rather than simply dividing headcount across shifts.

My principle

Capacity should follow demand and risk. Equal headcount does not necessarily create equal coverage.

Five dimensions

I assess demand, complexity, priority, capability and coverage before designing rotas or account allocation.

Inputs I use

Ticket patterns, priority mix, average handling time, backlog, skill requirements, account importance, escalation load, absence, channels and time zones all inform the model.

Workforce decisions

I use the evidence to determine staffing windows, skill coverage, escalation ownership, account allocation and where automation or process improvement can remove avoidable demand.

Leadership connection

Workforce planning is not only a scheduling exercise. It affects customer experience, employee pressure, SLA performance and the team's ability to work proactively.

Continuous review

I review capacity against actual demand and service outcomes, then adjust the model as customer behaviour, product usage and business priorities change.
