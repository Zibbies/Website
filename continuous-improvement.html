Portfolio case study

Continuous Improvement

Using structured problem solving to remove friction from the customer operation.

My improvement cycle

Define → Measure → Analyse → Improve → Control → Learn.

Tools I use

I draw from Pareto analysis, 5 Whys, process mapping, cause and effect analysis, FMEA and DMAIC depending on the problem.

Finding the real problem

I separate symptoms from causes. A rising ticket queue may be a staffing issue, but it may also be caused by a confusing process, product defect, poor knowledge, routing failure or repeated customer rework.

Improvement options

The answer can be process redesign, SOP changes, intelligent routing, automation, training, knowledge improvements, product changes or better customer communication.

Control

Every improvement needs an owner, metric, review cadence, threshold, escalation path and documentation so that the result does not disappear after the initial project.

My operating belief

I do not believe every customer problem needs a bigger team. Sometimes the best CX intervention is removing the reason the customer needed the team in the first place.
