Lean & Six Sigma Transformation Architecture

I approach customer operations through a rigorous continuous improvement lens. Instead of scaling up head counts to survive expanding contact volumes, I apply data discipline to target and remove structural waste, friction, and defects at their root cause:

Core Operating Paradigms

Capacity Allocation & Workforce Strategy

I view agent scheduling as a dynamic capacity optimization issue rather than a simple rota assignment. Rota design must balance ticket patterns, severity variables, account risk, individual capability profiles, and time-zone coverage. True operational stability means ensuring capacity follows business risk—not just spreading ticket counts equally.

Proactive Account Health Management

True customer success requires tracking leading behavior indicators instead of waiting for user complaints. By analyzing metrics like reopening patterns, repeat contact markers, transaction failure flags, and renewal timelines, I implement early intervention strategies to categorize accounts dynamically: Stable → Watch → At Risk → Intervention.
