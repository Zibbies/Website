My perspective

Customer experience is an operating discipline.

Customers experience the product, the process, the communication, the handoffs and the people behind them. I focus on the operating environment that creates that experience.

I am interested in what happens behind the experience: the people, processes, systems, decisions and customer signals that determine whether an organisation simply responds to demand or learns from it.
